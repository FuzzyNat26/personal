# FindBack.

**FindBack.** is an app that can store logistics (items) positions in the bag, to make it easier for Campers to check and find the items they are looking for.

<!-- truncate -->

**Features :**

1. Users can add their bags, compartments, and items into the app.
2. Users can update and delete their existing bags, compartments, and items.
3. Users can use their iPhone camera to take pictures of their bags and compartments.
4. Users can search their items with searching results consisting of:
    - Bag image
    - Bag Name
    - Compartment Name
    - Item Name
5. User's data are stored using Core Data.

## Problem Statement:

Camping is one of the recreational activities by settling or staying in the wild for several days that requires planning. Without one type of planning, namely appropriate logistics, campers can experience several obstacles such as running out or shortage of logistics supplies. These things can pose risks such as cold, being attacked by insects or snakes, even getting caught in a trivial accident that ends in critical.

The obstacles faced by the campers can be overcome with careful logistical planning such as:

-   Take notes of things to bring
-   Calculate daily needs
-   Planning logistics backup

With more careful planning, campers can get closer to nature and also have fun with their families

## Challenges:

**Assist logistical planning for "campers in nature with friends or family with the purpose of having fun"**
