**Joury** is an iOS app that accommodates and assists travelers with an easy-to-use recommendation list based on their purpose during travel.

<!-- truncate -->

## Problem Statement:

**From our investigation,** we empathised that campers and travelers want to pack their logistics simple but more structured way

**Because** they forget that they need these items and do not know what items they should bring to these and its annoying

**What if we could** provide an easy-to-use list and recommendations for the activities they would do

**For** campers and travelers

**So that** they can create a simple and quick list based on their activities and no more items drop behind

## Challenges:

**Level up FindBack by expand target users and enrich user experience in planning, organizing and checking their logistics**
