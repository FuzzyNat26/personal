**Eatsie** is an iOS app that helps parents with 1-2 year old children who have difficulties eating, by providing various recipes so that parents have ideas to explore children's appetite.

**Eatsie** recommends personalized recipes using CreateML to learn the child's preferences based on main ingredients, color, cooking process, and eating time.

<!-- truncate -->

## Problem Statement:

**From our investigation**, we learnt that Parents with children 1 - 2 years are worried that their child's nutrition and development will be disturbed due to difficulty eating caused by lack of interest in appearance and lack of variety of food.

**What if we could** help parents by providing a guide to cook attractive, varied food for kids and also easily available and nutritionally ingredients.
**So that parents** can provide food that attracts children to eat and has an impact on achieving children's growth and development targets according to their age.

## Challenges:

**Helping parents accompany the journey of children aged 1-5 in achieving balanced nutrition**
