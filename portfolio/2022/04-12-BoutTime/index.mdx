**BoutTime** is an app for College Students who want to play (enjoy themselves) while finishing their priorities.

**BoutTime** gamify the process by giving users points to level up after they able to accomplish their priorities.

<!-- truncate -->

## Problem Statement:

Students in big cities experience lack of efficiency and effectiveness in carrying out daily activities and busyness due to lack of motivation and do not know priorities in implementing time management.

Thus, students want to be disciplined in implementing priorities in time management.

Student time management can be managed through discipline in determining and implementing goals and priorities, as well as being supported by motivation and having fun in working on priorities.

One example that can be found is by prioritizing important things to do, such as campus project deadlines compared to going to the mall (4D method).

## Challenges:

**Help manage motivation and priorities for time management**
